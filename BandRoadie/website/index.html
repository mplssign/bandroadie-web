Dashboard

Open the app and know exactly what's coming up.

The dashboard puts your most important band information front and center — next rehearsal, upcoming gigs, potential bookings, and quick actions. No digging. Just open it and go.

  • ✓ Next rehearsal date, time, location, and attached setlist
  • ✓ Upcoming gigs listed in order with date and time
  • ✓ Potential gigs shown so the whole band sees the pipeline
  • ✓ Quick actions: Add Event · Create Setlist
  • ✓ Real-time — every member sees the same view
Add & Edit Events

Add a gig, rehearsal, or block-out in seconds.

One form handles everything. Choose Rehearsal, Gig, or Block Out — fill in the details and save. Your whole band sees it immediately. Need to change something? Open the event, edit, and hit Update. Done.

  • ✓ Rehearsals with location, duration, setlist, and recurrence
  • ✓ Gigs with venue, city, load-in time, pay, and setlist
  • ✓ Block-out dates with start/end and optional reason
  • ✓ Make any rehearsal recurring — weekly, biweekly, or monthly
  • ✓ Edit any field at any time — updates appear for everyone instantly
Potential Gigs

Track gigs you're working on, not just the ones confirmed.

Mark a gig as "potential" while you're waiting on confirmation. Members get an RSVP popup to say they're available before anything is locked in. Add multiple date options. When the gig confirms, flip the toggle and it goes live for everyone.

  • ✓ Mark any gig as potential — separate from confirmed events
  • ✓ Members confirm availability before you commit
  • ✓ Add multiple date options for the same gig
  • ✓ "Are you available?" popup sent to the whole band
  • ✓ Shows on the dashboard so everyone knows what's in the pipeline
  • ✓ Convert to confirmed with one toggle when it's locked in
Calendar & Block-out Dates

The full picture — gigs, rehearsals, and who's out of town.

Your band's calendar shows every scheduled event plus member block-out dates so you can see who's available before booking anything. Subscribe via iCal and your schedule syncs directly into Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.

  • ✓ Month view with gigs, rehearsals, and block-out dates
  • ✓ Members add block-out dates with start, end, and reason
  • ✓ Subscribe via iCal — syncs to Apple, Google, or Outlook
  • ✓ Choose which event types appear in your external calendar
  • ✓ Stays in sync automatically as events change
Contacts & Venues

Your band's contact book, built right into the app.

Stop scrolling your personal contacts for a venue's load-in number. BandRoadie stores your band members, your regular venues, and anyone else you work with — all in one place, accessible to the whole band.

  • ✓ Band member directory with instruments, phone, email, address
  • ✓ Venue directory with address and phone per venue
  • ✓ Add bookers, agents, and contacts per venue
  • ✓ Separate from your personal contacts — band-only
  • ✓ Available to every member of the band
